Ongoing collaboration · UX/UI

Yoolmi

A first encounter with Mexican Sign Language.

I’m designing the interface while investigating who the product should serve and what learning need it can meaningfully address.

View screens & decisions ↓Research direction ↓

Dogo Creative Studio + Fatima & Anapau

The challenge
Define the user problem behind the ambition to teach LSM.
My role
UX/UI, flows and research in progress.
Current status
Figma designs · Unity beta in development.
01 / Context

The idea came before the user problem.

Fatima and Anapau started Yoolmi with the ambition of making LSM learning more accessible. I joined later through Dogo Creative Studio, working alongside the founders, two developers and a 3D artist.

The design challenge: the idea came before a clearly defined user problem. My role includes improving the UI and questioning the assumptions behind it: who needs this, in what setting, and what would a useful first learning outcome look like?
02 / Learning interface

A clear next step, from home to practice.

These screens show the current visual direction and interaction structure. They are design snapshots; their usability and learning impact still need to be tested.

03 / Beyond the happy path

Support before and after a wrong turn.

Account creation and recovery also shape the experience. These proposed states explore adult involvement, consent and a way back after a login error.

04 / Research direction

The questions shaping the next iteration.

The current interface is a starting point. I’m investigating the learning context before treating its underlying assumptions as product requirements.

01Learning independently

Working assumption
The current interface proposes a route a child can follow.
Investigate
Observe how a learner starts an activity and when they need help.
Possible design change
Adjust instructions, pacing and support.

02The adult’s role

Working assumption
The account flow proposes adult help during registration.
Investigate
Understand with families and teachers who supports learning and in what setting.
Possible design change
Revisit profiles, account ownership and moments of support.

03A useful first outcome

Working assumption
The current question focuses on recognizing a sign.
Investigate
Define a realistic first learning goal with people who use LSM.
Possible design change
Adapt activities and feedback to the learning goal.
Current outcome
A set of interface and account-flow proposals, with research still in progress. Learning gains and usability improvements have not yet been established.

My contribution: UX/UI and flows. Original concept: Fatima and Anapau. Development: Joan and Daniel. 3D: Arael. Character and environment artwork belong to the project team.
