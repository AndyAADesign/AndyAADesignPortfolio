How I work

Design the system, not just the screen.

I like starting with the thing someone is actually trying to do. From there I can organize the information, reduce friction, prototype the interaction and test whether it behaves the way people expect.

QA changed how I design. I notice edge cases, implementation constraints and all the places where a clean mockup can fall apart once a real person starts clicking around.

Background

I know what happens after the mockup.

UX/UI and interaction design are the core of my work, but I’ve spent enough time in QA, game production, front-end and 3D to understand what other disciplines need from a design. That makes collaboration easier and usually catches problems earlier.
