How I work

Systems first.

My strongest work usually sits where design decisions depend on understanding the system underneath the interface. I enjoy breaking complicated interactions into clear rules, testing them with people and iterating until the experience feels natural.

My QA background makes me especially attentive to edge cases, implementation constraints, reproducibility and what actually happens when real users interact with a product rather than what a mockup assumes they will do.
