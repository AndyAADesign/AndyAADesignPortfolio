How I work

Design the system, not just the screen.

I approach UX as a systems problem: understand what the user is trying to do, organize the information and interactions around that goal, then test whether the experience actually behaves the way people expect.

My QA background makes me especially attentive to edge cases, implementation constraints, reproducibility and the gap between what a mockup assumes will happen and what real users actually do.
